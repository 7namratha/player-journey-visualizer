New User Experience Guide

Use this guide to set your scope, explore player movement, compare signals, and control playback—all in one place.

01 · Set your scope

  1. Choose a Time period (Day, Week, Month, Year or Custom range).
  2. Select a Map. Choose one map to inspect player positions; All Maps shows a per-map summary because map coordinates differ.
  3. Use Match search to find a match ID, then choose Player to view everyone, humans, bots, or one player.
  4. Use Reset in the top bar to return to the default filters.

02 · Read and explore the map

  • Human players are round tokens; bots are teal diamonds. Trails show movement and event markers show signals.
  • Choose a Map layer to focus on paths, kills, deaths, or traffic. Use Smooth or 10×10 grid for heatmap presentation.
  • Toggle Signals to show or hide kills, deaths, storm deaths, loot, and other events. Event density adjusts signal visibility.
  • Turn on Probe, then drag a circle on the map to measure activity in a zone. Use Grid and Follow when useful.

03 · Play back a journey

Use the timeline at the bottom to scrub through the match. The transport controls start, pause, step, or jump to the beginning/end. With All Matches selected, journeys are overlaid and aligned at 00:00.

Positions use world X and Z mapped through each map’s origin and scale; world Y is elevation. Time is shown as mm:ss from each journey’s start. Source positions are sampled approximately every 5 seconds.
